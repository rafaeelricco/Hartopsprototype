Hart Agency Visual Language

Every token, pattern, and component we ship across Hart Ops and Educator Mobile, on one scrollable page.

Brand

Positioning, logo lockup, and the iconography contract. One brand color, Lucide icons only.

Color

One brand burgundy paired with the Tailwind Gray ramp. Four semantic roles plus soft tinted badges.

Typography

Inter only. 400 body, 500 UI chrome, 600 titles & metrics. Six-step scale from 12 to 24.

Layout

4px spacing base, the 8/10/12/16 radius scale, and elevation — flat by default; shadows only on overlays.

Components

Buttons, inputs, cards, badges, and the shadcn-parallel sidebar nav — all Elevation-0 with the 1-pixel rule.

Educator Mobile patterns

Field-facing PWA companion to Hart Ops. Same tokens, different frame and motion vocabulary. The mobile surface isn't yet wired into /app/frontend/ — treat the spec as canonical.
